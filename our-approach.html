Precision Logistics Framework

How We Ensure Flawless, Deliberate Execution

Good logistics is never accidental. We combine advance customs filings, strict route planning, and hands-on telematics to safeguard every shipment from delay.

The Katozi Method

Good Logistics Starts with Listening.

You deserve complete visibility over what happens to your shipment. We keep the entire process structured, personalized, and transparent from day one.

01
Assessment & Tariff Classification

We evaluate your cargo dimensions, hazardous requirements, commercial invoices, and verify correct East African Community (EAC) HS codes for accurate duty assessment.

02
Strategic Routing & Carrier Allotment

We select optimal flight connections or ocean vessels, negotiate space allotments, secure container equipment, and establish departure milestones.

03
Advance Customs Lodging (Zero Demurrage)

Declarations are lodged with the Uganda Revenue Authority (URA) and port authorities before your vessel or aircraft touches down, preventing port storage penalties.

04
Live Telematics & Transit Monitoring

Your cargo is continuously monitored via GPS and regional electronic tracking seals as it moves along arterial roads or transfers across border inspection stations.

05
Proof of Delivery & Account Clearance

Final delivery to your factory, warehouse, or commercial site with signed receipt, condition inspection, and transparent itemized billing reconciliation.

🛡️ Safety & Risk Mitigation Protocol

Every shipment handled by Katozi Logistics is governed by strict chain-of-custody protocols, tamper-evident seals, and optional comprehensive transit insurance to protect your financial capital against any unforeseen eventuality.

Operational Standards

How We Guard Your Time and Capital

Four non-negotiable operational commitments we uphold for every client, regardless of shipment size.

Proactive Delay Prevention

We actively monitor port congestion levels, weather patterns, and border post clearance queues at Malaba and Busia to reroute or expedite consignments ahead of time.

Document Pre-Audit

Incomplete paperwork is the #1 cause of customs delays. Our documentation team reviews all commercial invoices, packing lists, and origin certificates prior to shipping departure.

Direct WhatsApp Communication

You speak directly with the officer physically present at Entebbe airport or the Mombasa clearing terminal. No call-center transfers, just immediate, actionable answers.

Daily Milestone Reporting

Receive clear status notifications at every milestone: cargo uplift, international arrival, customs release, road dispatch, and border clearance.
