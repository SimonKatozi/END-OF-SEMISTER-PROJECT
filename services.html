Ocean Freight (FCL & LCL)

Global ocean transport for bulk commercial goods. We manage full container loads (20ft, 40ft, 40ft HC) and shared container consolidation (LCL) via Mombasa and Dar es Salaam.

  • FCL Full Container Load management
  • LCL Less than Container Load consolidation
  • Port handling & transit bond execution

03 / OVERLAND ROAD

Regional Road Haulage

Dependable overland trucking along East Africa's Northern and Central transport corridors. Seamless deliveries connecting Mombasa/Dar ports with Uganda and neighbouring states.

  • GPS telematics & RECTS tracking
  • Cross-border convoy & escort coordination
  • Border clearance at Malaba, Busia & Mutukula
  • Direct factory & warehouse delivery
Book Road Transport
04 / CUSTOMS BROKERAGE

Customs Clearance & Brokerage

Licensed Uganda Revenue Authority (URA) clearing agents. We manage tariff classifications, electronic declarations, duty evaluations, and prompt cargo release.

  • Single Customs Territory (SCT) declarations
  • Tariff code (HS) verification & tax advice
  • Bond execution & duty exemption handling
  • UNBS / PVoC inspection clearance
Consult Customs Desk

Cargo Handling & Warehousing

Keep your commercial inventory secure. We offer bonded and non-bonded warehouse storage in Kampala with forklift handling, sorting, and final dispatch.

  • Secure bonded holding yards
  • De-consolidation & palletization
  • Inventory tracking & reporting
  • Final-mile commercial distribution
