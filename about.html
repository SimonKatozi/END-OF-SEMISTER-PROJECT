Our Story

A Dependable Logistics Partner for Every Shipment.

Katozi Logistics & Clearing Company was established to solve one of the greatest challenges facing businesses in East Africa: delays, uncertainty, and fragmented communication in freight shipping and customs clearance.

From our headquarters in Kampala and operational clearance offices at Entebbe International Airport, the Port of Mombasa, and Port of Dar es Salaam, we bring air, ocean, and overland freight into a unified, transparent journey.

Every consignment represents a client's hard-earned business promise. That is why we listen first, verify cargo requirements, and deploy customized freight strategies that safeguard cargo integrity and deliver on time.

  • ✓Practical multimodal support across air, sea, and overland road transport
  • ✓Proactive clearance procedures that prevent costly port demurrage and penalties
  • ✓Tailored logistics planning designed around your specific schedule and cargo needs
