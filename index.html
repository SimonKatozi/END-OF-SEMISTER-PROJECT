Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers on customs clearance, transit times, shipping documentation, and rates.

To process customs clearance under the Uganda Revenue Authority (URA), you typically need: the Commercial Invoice, Packing List, Bill of Lading (for sea freight) or Air Waybill (for air cargo), Certificate of Origin, and where applicable, a Certificate of Conformity (PVoC/Uganda National Bureau of Standards). Katozi assists with reviewing and preparing all necessary documentation.

Direct air freight from Dubai generally arrives in Entebbe within 2 to 3 days. From major Chinese hubs (Guangzhou, Shanghai), transit takes between 3 to 5 business days depending on flight connections. Once landed at Entebbe, our clearing desk can finalize customs release within 24 to 48 hours.

Demurrage occurs when containers stay at the port beyond the shipping line's free days. Katozi uses electronic advance lodging with KRA and URA so that import declarations and duty assessments are prepared while the ship is still at sea. The moment the vessel berths, we initiate fast-track clearance and haulage dispatch.

Yes. Katozi Logistics operates active cross-border corridors under the Single Customs Territory. We handle regional transit bonds, convoy coordination, and border crossing clearance at Elegu (for South Sudan), Katuna/Cyanika (for Rwanda), and Mpondwe/Bunagana (for Eastern DRC).
